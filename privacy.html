Privacy Policy

Yusuf's Preview

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Yusuf's Preview does not collect any data. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own. Your documents and everything you do with them stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account, which only you can access.

What the app stores, and where

Your documents

When you add a PDF, the app copies it into its own folder in your iCloud Drive. That folder is visible to you in the Files app under "Yusuf's Preview". The original file you imported from is never modified. Your markup is written into these copies when you save.

Your place in each document

The page you were reading, the chapters you create, your recently opened list, and a note of where a document was imported from are stored in your iCloud key-value storage. This is what lets you pick up on another device where you left off. It is part of your personal iCloud account.

Editable markup

A copy of your pen strokes is kept on the device itself, in the app's Application Support folder, so strokes can be erased and edited individually after you reopen a document. This never leaves the device.

Settings

Your preferences — such as which controls stay on screen and whether markup is limited to Apple Pencil — are stored on the device.

iCloud

The app uses Apple's iCloud Drive and iCloud key-value storage so your library is the same on every device you sign in to. This data is held in your own iCloud account under your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it and no way to read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you turn iCloud off for this app, it will keep working with whatever is already on that device.

Handoff

If you have Handoff enabled, the app tells your other nearby Apple devices which document you have open and which page you are on, so you can continue reading on another device. This is handled entirely by Apple between devices signed in to your Apple Account. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What is never collected

  • No personal information, name, email address or account of any kind
  • No usage analytics, crash reporting or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any sort
  • No contents of your documents — the app cannot see them and never transmits them
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13. There is no sign-up, no messaging and no user-generated content shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the markup sidecar and settings held on that device. Your documents live in your iCloud Drive; you can delete them at any time from the Files app or from within the app. Removing the app's data from iCloud settings clears the saved reading positions and chapters.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
